In plain English, here’s exactly what we’re building and what a store owner gets at the end.

---

### The tool in one sentence
A free, no-sign-up webpage where a Shopify store owner types in their store URL, waits 10–15 seconds, and then sees a full report showing **how much money their slow-loading store is costing them every single month**.

---

### The end result the user sees (the “Report”) contains:

**1. Overall Speed Score (0–100)**  
A big, colored number (red, yellow, or green) that instantly tells them if their store is “Good,” “Needs Work,” or “Poor” — just like a school grade.

**2. Three Core Web Vitals cards**  
- **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint):** How long until the main content loads.  
- **INP (Interaction to Next Paint):** How fast the store responds to taps and clicks.  
- **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift):** How much things jump around as the page loads.  
Each card shows the raw value (e.g., “5.2 s”), and a clearly labeled status: Good / Needs Work / Poor.

**3. The “Revenue Leak” Calculator**  
This is the big payoff. The store owner can adjust three sliders or number fields:
- Monthly visitors  
- Average order value  
- Current conversion rate  

Then the tool instantly calculates:
- Their current monthly revenue  
- What their revenue **could be** if their store loaded as fast as a modern headless store (using the same traffic and order value)  
- The **exact dollar amount** they’re losing every month because of the current slow load time  
- That same loss projected out to a full year  

The number is displayed in huge, red, can’t-ignore-it text.

**4. A Benchmark Chart**  
A simple horizontal bar chart showing their mobile and desktop scores side-by-side with the average scores of a fast Next.js headless storefront. This makes the performance gap obvious at a glance.

**5. A Booking Call-to-Action**  
At the bottom, a button like “Book a Free 20-Min Strategy Call” that links to your calendar. This is where the store owner becomes a lead.

---

### What the store owner walks away with
They leave with a crystal-clear, personalized answer to one question: **“How much money is my slow Shopify store actually leaking?”** — not a vague warning, but a dollar figure tied to their own traffic and prices. That’s the whole product.
